The Heart Behind
Visioners

I started designing in 2013, long before Visioners existed. I was just a teenager volunteering at my church, creating slides, flyers, and whatever was needed for Sunday. I didn’t know it then, but those small acts of service were planting the seed of a much bigger vision: that creativity and excellence could preach the Gospel.

By 2019, I began working exclusively with churches and Christian organizations, helping pastors and communication teams bring their vision to life through design that truly connects with people.

Around that time, God began stirring a dream in my heart, a vision for Visioners: a movement of creatives using design and storytelling to serve the Church with beauty and purpose.

In 2020, that dream became a reality. What began with one designer became a team passionate about helping ministries bring their God-given vision to life.

For three years, I served as Design Director at Union Church, one of the fastest-growing churches in America. Ranked #1 in 2021, #1 again in 2024, and #5 in 2025. Leading design at Union taught me how to build systems and stories that support real growth without losing spiritual authenticity.

Today, Visioners partners with churches across the U.S. and beyond as their creative department, helping them move from scattered visuals to cohesive storytelling that inspires faith, unity, and action.

At Visioners, we don’t just make things look good. We make them mean something — to build movements that outlast us all.

And this is just the beginning.
